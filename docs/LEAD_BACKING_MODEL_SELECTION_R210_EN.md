# Lead/Backing model selection

- **Primary:** UVR KARA 2, only after independently documented rights and operator double confirmation. Commercial rights are not automatically established.
- **Secondary:** MVSep Mega 53 v1.0.21. The author expressly granted MIT rights for the weights including commercial redistribution: https://github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training/issues/245 . Training-data rights are not warranted. Operator-supplied upstream runtime, configuration and original SHA-256-verified checkpoint are required.
- **Multiple singers:** singer-conditioned SAM Audio remains a separate workflow. Mega 53 produces aggregate `lead-vocal` and `back-vocal` stems; it does not identify N individual singers.
- No real-model audio quality benchmark was run in this cycle.
