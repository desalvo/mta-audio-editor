# MTA Audio Editor commercial service terms

**Draft subject to legal review.** Operator identity, contacts, effective date, pricing and jurisdiction must be configured before commercial activation.

## 1. Service description
The operator offers access to CPU, GPU, storage and audio processing facilities in exchange for payment. Prices may include a commercial margin.

## 2. Software licensing
MTA Audio Editor remains distributed under its open-source licenses; payments relate to the hosted service and do not transfer software component rights.

## 3. Registration
Access may require registration and approval, subject to published eligibility, limits and applicable law.

## 4. Pricing transparency
CPU, GPU, storage, subscription, quota and additional service prices must be disclosed before purchase, including mandatory taxes and fees where applicable.

## 5. Subscriptions
Duration, pricing, renewals, any automatic renewal, included resources and cancellation procedures must be explicit and compliant with applicable law.

## 6. Credits
Value, use, any expiration and refund conditions for credits must be disclosed before purchase. Mandatory user rights remain protected.

## 7. Accounting
Technical consumption is captured in a shared ledger independent of pricing; rates and payment transactions are separate from usage measurement.

## 8. AI licenses
Only components licensed for the intended commercial use may run. Checks cover code, checkpoints, voicebanks, soundfonts, dependencies, remote inference and redistribution rights.

## 9. Suspension, adjustments and refunds
Suspensions and refunds follow published conditions and mandatory law; disabling a model does not extinguish accrued rights or balances.

## 10. Privacy and liability
The operator must publish contact, privacy, complaint, liability and consumer-rights information, including withdrawal rights where applicable.

## 11. Changes
Material changes are announced as required by law; accrued rights remain protected.
