# Nonprofit shared infrastructure terms

**Draft terms subject to legal review.** Effective date: to be set by the operator.

## 1. Purpose and access
The server operates without profit for individually authorized registered users. Admission may be limited by available resources. Access does not constitute a purchase of a commercial AI processing service.

## 2. Software
MTA Audio Editor and its native applications are free of charge under their applicable licenses. Use of external infrastructure does not change the software licenses.

## 3. Contributions and eligible costs
Contributions cover only actual, documented and relevant hardware, depreciation, power, cooling, storage, maintenance and repair costs, subject to applicable rules. No profit margin or arbitrary surcharge is permitted.

## 4. Resource accounting
CPU, GPU, storage and other relevant consumption is measured in a technical ledger independent from billing. Allocation methods must be documented and transparent; each user can inspect their own consumption.

## 5. Periodic statements
No prepaid credits or subscriptions are offered. Contributions are calculated only after the reporting period, monthly by default, itemizing consumption, methods, costs, adjustments and allocations. A technical statement is not automatically a tax invoice.

## 6. No profit and reconciliation
Total contributions must not exceed recoverable eligible costs. Excess collections are reconciled through refunds or reductions in later cost-sharing statements, not converted into commercial purchasing credits.

## 7. AI licensing
Use remains subject to the licenses of code, checkpoints, voicebanks, soundfonts and dependencies. Nonprofit status does not automatically permit remote inference, redistribution or third-party use. Components without verified sufficient rights remain unavailable.

## 8. User obligations
Users respect licenses, capacity limitations, and rights in their content; resale of server access is prohibited. Purpose declarations may be required where model licenses demand them.

## 9. Audit and liability
The operator retains auditable cost, usage, adjustment and control records to prevent double charges or overcollection. The operator and users remain responsible for their respective activities under applicable law.

## 10. Changes
Material changes will be communicated and, where needed, newly accepted. Mandatory law and non-waivable rights prevail.
